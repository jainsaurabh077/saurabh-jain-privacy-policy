Privacy Policy — Saurabh Jain Digital Product Publisher

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Saurabh Jain Digital Product Publisher handles information when it connects to Pinterest to publish Pins for Saurabh Jain's own Pinterest account.

Information we access

With your authorization, the app may access basic Pinterest account information, board names and IDs, Pin content and metadata, publishing status, and basic Pin performance information.

How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate the Pinterest account, publish original digital-product Pins to selected boards, maintain a publishing queue, prevent duplicates, and review basic performance.

What we do not do

We do not ask for or store your Pinterest password. We do not sell personal information, use Pinterest data for advertising, or access unrelated Pinterest accounts.

Storage and sharing

Authorization data and workflow records are protected with access controls. Information is shared only with Pinterest and services needed to operate the publishing workflow, or when required by law.

Deletion and revocation

You may revoke Pinterest authorization at any time. You may also request deletion of app records by contacting us. Revoking access stops future Pinterest actions but does not remove Pins already published.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, email jainsaurabh013@gmail.com.
